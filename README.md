### Date: October 2025


___

### Challenge: Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Space Biology Research



___


#### An AI assistant that helps with answering questions related to Biology in space questions, and eases the understanding with image retrieval. The data was collected from over 600 Space biology publications published since 2010. It uses Gemini-2.5-flash to answer questions based on retrieved documents related to the question asked, images are provided as well from Nasa recourses.
